How to play the word game

The word game is broken up between 3 difficulty modes (easy, medium, and hard). Each mode will have its own unique timer for the game to challenge you to be quick on your feet as some job roles require pristine time management. The game will ask you 10 questions pertaining to various IT jobs in the job market and score you according to the difficulty mode you selected.

Easy Mode: 3 mins

Medium Mode: 2 mins

Hard Mode: 1 mins

All questions are multiple choice.
