Welcome to JobHopper an interactive app that is here to help you along on your path to reach the next level in tech. JobHopper pairs and shares careers with you based on a WordCloud that is created when you sign up. Once registered you will be able to see the WordCloud filled with various IT careers based on your specifications. As a registered user you will be able to play our Job Quiz which is a multiple choice word game. Click on the Rules to see the rules and set up of the game.
